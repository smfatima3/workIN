Two tasks showed a causal effect.

For Qwen3-14B, a stale glass position caused spills and a stale belt speed caused drops. On moving trays, correcting the remembered load increased overloads. The study tests high-level decisions under fixed controllers. Transfer to articulated-arm manipulation, hardware damage, and human injury remains untested.
